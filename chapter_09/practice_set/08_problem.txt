


**🌟 "The Journey" 🌟**

In the quiet of the morning light,
Dreams take wings and hearts take flight.
Steps may stumble, roads may bend,
But every turn’s a chance to mend.

The sun may hide, the skies may gray,
Yet hope will guide us through the day.
With courage deep and vision wide,
We walk with strength, with stars as guide.

So rise, be bold, and chase the flame—
For life is more than just a name.
